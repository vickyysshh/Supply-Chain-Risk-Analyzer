   ✓ Port charges: $2,500-$3,200 each

   ✓ Customs duty: $1,200 (estimate)
   ✓ Risk multiplier: +5-30% based on risk

   Expected range: $9,000-$12,000+

   - Shortest practical route
   - Best efficiency score
   - Balanced risk & cost

   Route 2: ALTERNATIVE 1 (Safer)
   - Longer but lower risk
   - Better for stormy season
   - Recommended if HIGH risk detected

   Route 3: ALTERNATIVE 2 (Faster/Cheaper)
   - Fastest transit time
   - May have geopolitical risks
   - Use if cost is primary concern
